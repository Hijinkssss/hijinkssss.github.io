## Design reference

Robin Thomasson, **Helldivers II – Design System and Style Guide Overview**, published May 18, 2025:
https://www.behance.net/gallery/225589313/Helldivers-II-Design-System-and-Style-Guide-Overview

The user supplied `HD2.rar` containing eight WebP style-guide boards. They were inspected as visual references. Only a text-free environment crop from board 1 is shipped as `assets/images/deployment-scene.webp`; the full boards, explanatory prose, sample UI screens, and official emblems are not redistributed in the site.

Archive SHA-256: `6b299ce20a94cc43ea9770b760370489e9f6e119e3c84432bd7062476d0c47f6`.

The uploaded reference establishes provenance, not a new license for third-party game artwork. No third-party asset is represented as independently created or freely licensed. Replace the environmental crop with your own imagery if different asset permissions are needed for another use.
